# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

New interns at EXZY Co., Ltd., accessed during their first days and weeks. Primarily Thai university students on a mini-internship program. They arrive on day one — nervous, unfamiliar with the office, needing quick answers. Primary jobs: find the Day 1 checklist, understand company structure, look up policies, and access resources like the timesheet guide. The HR team uses an admin panel to update showcase content. Both Thai and English reading comfort assumed.

## Product Purpose

The EXZY Intern Handbook is the single source of truth for interns — everything they need to navigate their internship in one place: Day 1 guide, orientation PDF, org chart, work policies, Wi-Fi credentials, meeting room info, timesheet walkthrough, and intern showcase. Success means an intern can land on this site on day one and immediately feel oriented, informed, and welcomed — no emailing HR for basic info.

## Brand Personality

Modern, Friendly, Professional. Voice is warm but authoritative — like a prepared senior colleague, not a corporate brochure. Bilingual (Thai primary, English secondary). Should feel like a well-designed company onboarding experience, not a PDF scan or a startup pitch deck.

## Anti-references

- **Over-designed SaaS landing pages**: no hero metric grids, no gradient card carousels, no "10x your productivity" energy. This is a guide, not a sales page.
- **Wikipedia / plain docs**: needs clear visual hierarchy, brand identity, and scannable structure. Walls of unstyled text fail interns who are scanning for a quick answer.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity first** — interns are stressed on day one; every page must be scannable within seconds. Structure beats decoration.
2. **Company pride without the pitch** — EXZY's brand should feel present and polished without crossing into marketing. This is internal, not a landing page.
3. **Bilingual by design** — Thai is the primary language; English labels appear throughout. Both must read naturally at all sizes, with appropriate line-height for Thai script.
4. **Handbook structure is the UX** — the site has chapters (Day 1, Orientation, Company, Policy, Resources, Showcase). Visual structure should mirror that mental model so navigation feels obvious.
5. **Welcoming reduces friction** — the reader is new and may be anxious. Design should lower the barrier to finding information, not add visual noise that slows them down.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Thai language (lang="th") requires generous line-height (1.75+ for body, 1.6+ for headings to accommodate tone marks). Reduced-motion preference must be respected for all animations. Color contrast: body text ≥ 4.5:1 against background. Navigation must be keyboard-accessible.
